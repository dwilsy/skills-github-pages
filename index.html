Who We Are

The Team

With over 40 members, dedicated mentors, and supportive sponsors, Walton Robotics is a powerhouse in the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) sphere.

The Build

We spend January through February utilizing mechanical assembly, CAD, and 3D printing to build highly competitive robots for the state and world championships.

The Community

Operating out of our dedicated build site on the Walton High School campus, we are entirely funded by our non-profit foundation, parents, and corporate grants.

Community & Outreach

Destination Engineering

We open our full-scale practice field to area teams, providing a collaborative space to fine-tune robot driving skills and iterate on programming during the competition season.

GirlsFIRST & STEM Camps

Inspiring the next generation through hands-on summer camps, FLL tournaments, and targeted GirlsFIRST initiatives to close the gender gap in STEM.

FTC Pipelines

We sponsor FIRST Tech Challenge programs for middle school students, ensuring younger innovators have a direct pathway into high school competitive robotics.
